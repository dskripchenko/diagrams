Edges no longer cut the corner of a node they pass: beside a node, an edge goes through the node's rank straight and bends only in the gap between ranks. Frame titles (subgraphs, packages, namespaces) are drawn above the edges with a halo of the frame's fill, so an edge entering a frame passes under its title instead of striking it out.
