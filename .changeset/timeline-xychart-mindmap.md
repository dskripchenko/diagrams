Three new diagram types: `timeline` (periods, several events per period, sections coloured like Mermaid's), `xychart-beta` / `xychart` (bar and line series over a categorical or numeric x axis, vertical or horizontal, automatic "nice" value ranges and tick labels, a legend for titled series) and `mindmap` (an indented outline with every Mermaid node shape, markdown labels, drawn as a balanced left/right tree with a colour per branch).
