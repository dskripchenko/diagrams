C4 diagrams: `C4Context`, `C4Container`, `C4Component`, `C4Dynamic` and `C4Deployment` in Mermaid's C4-PlantUML syntax — persons, systems, containers and components with their `Db`, `Queue` and `_Ext` variants, nested boundaries and deployment nodes, every `Rel` variant with `Rel_U/D/L/R` honoured as layout hints, `RelIndex` and automatic numbering in dynamic diagrams, `UpdateElementStyle`, `UpdateRelStyle` and `UpdateLayoutConfig`. Laid out like Mermaid — a grid per boundary honouring `c4ShapeInRow`/`c4BoundaryInRow` — with related elements placed side by side, orthogonal relations routed around shapes and frame titles, and labels kept clear of each other. Drawn in the classic C4 look, themeable through new `--dg-c4-*` variables with documented dark-theme values.
