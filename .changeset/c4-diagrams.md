C4 diagrams: `C4Context`, `C4Container`, `C4Component`, `C4Dynamic` and `C4Deployment` in Mermaid's C4-PlantUML syntax — persons, systems, containers and components with their `Db`, `Queue` and `_Ext` variants, nested boundaries and deployment nodes, every `Rel` variant with `Rel_U/D/L/R` honoured as layout hints, `RelIndex` and automatic numbering in dynamic diagrams, `UpdateElementStyle`, `UpdateRelStyle` and `UpdateLayoutConfig`. Drawn in the classic C4 look, themeable through new `--dg-c4-*` variables.
