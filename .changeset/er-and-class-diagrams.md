Two new diagram types. `erDiagram`: entities drawn as tables (type, name, PK/FK/UK keys and comments in aligned columns), aliases, relationships with every crow's foot cardinality in symbols or words, identifying and non-identifying lines, labels, self relationships, direction and styles. `classDiagram`: classes with attributes and methods (visibility, static and abstract members, parameters, return types, generics), annotations, every UML relationship with heads on either end, cardinalities and labels, namespaces, notes, direction, styles and links. Two new theme variables, `--dg-row-fill` and `--dg-row-alt-fill`, colour table rows and class compartments.
